## 0. Read first: rules and risks

1. **Check your current contract.** You are a consultant at Reliance Intelligence (Jio). Read your contract for moonlighting, conflict-of-interest and IP clauses before taking outside work. Never reuse employer code or data.
2. **Keep employer details generic.** The profiles below describe results ("a platform serving 500K+ daily users"). They don't name internal systems or share confidential architecture. Naming employers in the Employment history section is normal.
3. **Use one account per platform.** Duplicate accounts get banned on both Upwork and Fiverr.
4. **Don't put contact details in your profile or messages.** No email, phone, WhatsApp or LinkedIn URL. Both platforms enforce this. Keep all contact and payment inside the platform.
5. **Use your real name and a clear headshot.** Your `public/shailendra-profile.jpeg` works if it is a face-forward, well-lit, plain-background photo.
6. **Don't buy reviews or trade fake ones.** Every account that tried this got suspended.
